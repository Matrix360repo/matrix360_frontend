Frequently asked questions

Clear terms. Clear decisions.

Can we retain our existing CRM?

Yes, that is the target architecture. CRM sync and data import would be enabled only once approved connectors are implemented.

Is this a fully operational platform?

Not yet. This director-facing experience demonstrates product journeys and frontend behavior; backend capabilities are at different development stages.

Can agencies manage more than one client?

Multi-client workspaces are designed into the experience. Production tenant isolation and permissions remain release requirements.

Can AI send campaigns automatically?

Only in a future authorized service. Production campaign activation needs consent checks and appropriate human approval.
