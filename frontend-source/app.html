HELP, WHENEVER YOU NEED IT

Questions? Here are the simple answers.

Do I need marketing or technical experience?

No. Start by telling us what you sell and what you want to achieve. The workspace guides you to the right next step.

What can I do today?

You can explore the guided product demonstration. The authenticated CRM module is available where its backend has been configured and verified. Other modules show their integration status.

Will Matrix 360 send messages automatically?

No. This demonstration cannot send marketing communications. Any future outreach must respect consent and require authorized approval.

Is my business information saved?

The sample onboarding does not create a permanent customer account. Only authenticated modules with verified storage should be relied upon for persisted records.

Can I use this for real estate or manufacturing?

The platform is designed for both, with industry-specific language and journeys. Availability of live data processing and creative automation varies by module.

How do I log in or sign up?

Use Sign in to reach the CRM pilot login. Start free preview opens guided onboarding; general account registration is not yet connected, so no account is created by the preview.
