    A few useful answers

    You're in control.
    Every step.

    A growth platform should simplify decisions—not hide them.

    Is Matrix 360 a marketing agency or software platform?

    The vision combines both: agency-quality strategic thinking and creative capabilities delivered through an AI-native platform. Clients may eventually choose self-service tools or more managed assistance. Individual functions will become available as they are validated.

    Do I have to understand AI, CRM systems or automation?

    No. The intended experience begins with your commercial objective, not technical configuration. Behind the scenes, specialist capabilities can prepare options for your review.

    Will the platform automatically spend my budget or contact customers?

    Not through this concept website. The intended production system has explicit approval, provider-permission and spending controls before consequential actions. The example plan above only prepares text in your own browser.

    Which features are available today?

    Matrix 360 is under development. There are working engineering foundations for CRM, audience processing, content production and campaign simulation. A complete agent-coordinated, cross-platform growth service is the product direction, not yet an accepted live end-to-end capability.

    Can the platform adapt to different industries and locations?

    That is a core design objective. Your business identity, operating location, target markets and offers should guide your plan without limiting you to a fixed list of pilot industries or regions.
