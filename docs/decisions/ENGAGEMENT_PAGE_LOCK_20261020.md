## Identity and core proposition
**Matrix 360 — Your AI-Powered Marketing & Growth Team.**

Combine experienced marketing strategy with business intelligence, creativity, campaign planning and eventually authorized multi-agent operations. Present value to the business owner first, not the platform's modules or technical stack.

## Fixed customer journey
1. **Understand the benefits** — what business growth problems Matrix 360 addresses.
2. **Describe your business objective** — one natural-language prompt with useful examples and optional non-sensitive context.
3. **Receive a proposed growth plan** — a relevant, editable, evidence-bound plan with assumptions and honest capability statuses.
4. **Review and approve next steps** — the user remains in control of publishing, outreach, provider costs and material decisions.

## Benefit pillars
- Think smarter — market/competitor intelligence, strategy, positioning.
- Target better — audience discovery, responsible enrichment, explainable qualification.
- Create faster — brand-consistent content, visuals and authentic reel outputs.
- Stay organized — plan, budget recommendations and content calendar.
- Work together — human-reviewed, eventually multi-agent-coordinated workflows.
- Grow with insight — CRM, attributable results and informed improvement.

**Commercial promises:** better marketing decisions; more capability with less effort; greater accountability for growth.

## Non-negotiable UX requirements
- Customer-facing engagement, not another technical onboarding dashboard.
- Clear mobile experience with a single obvious initial action and no technical-jargon prerequisite.
- Flexible industries, objectives and target geographies; do not hardcode the three pilot clients.
- Preserve existing verified login, tenancy, CRM, upgraded dynamic business onboarding and active Copilot development.
- No unsupported claims: label actual, proposed, simulated and unavailable capabilities clearly.
- Only authenticated, authorized tenant membership determines business identity.
- Production releases, external communications and paid services still require separate approvals.

## Validation
For one first-time nontechnical user on phone and desktop, verify they can understand the value, enter an objective, reach the correct sign-in/plan review and distinguish what's live from what's planned. Capture an isolated PR/preview, screenshots, browser and regression results before requesting acceptance.

**Priority:** Preserve 22 October P0 acceptance. Engagement-page refinement is a following-stage deliverable; this decision does not trigger a production merge or cross-engine integration.
